INTRODUCTION
============

This document explains, in simple terms, every upgrade and new feature we
will be building into the school management system's result engine. The goal
is to make the system produce professional, comprehensive, and fully
customizable student result sheets that meet the standards of modern schools.

Everything described below is designed to be flexible. Each school using the
platform will be able to turn features on or off, adjust rules, and
customize what appears on their students' report cards — all from a simple
settings page, without needing any technical knowledge.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ADMISSION / REGISTRATION NUMBERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

Every student in a school has a unique admission number or registration
number. This is the official identifier that follows the student throughout
their time at the school. It is different from the system-generated ID that
the software uses internally. Think of it as the number printed on a
student's ID card and report card — the one the school recognises.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

The system will automatically generate admission numbers for every new
student. The format will be customizable by each school. For example, a
school called "Noplin Prestige School" might choose the format:

    NPS/2025/001
    NPS/2025/002
    NPS/2025/003

Where "NPS" is the school's abbreviation, "2025" is the year, and "001" is
the student's sequence number. The school can set the abbreviation, choose
whether to use a full year (2025) or short year (25), and pick the starting
number.

For schools that already have existing admission numbers from previous years
or a different system, they will not lose those numbers. They can simply
upload an Excel or CSV file containing their old student records with the
existing admission numbers, and the system will import and retain them
exactly as they are.

Going forward, any new student added to the system will automatically
receive the next number in the sequence, unless the admin manually types in
a specific number instead.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCHOOL FEES ON THE RESULT CARD
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

Many schools print a breakdown of next term's school fees directly on the
student's report card. This serves as both a notice and a record for
parents. Currently, the system has a fixed set of four fee types (Tuition,
Equipment, Library, and Sports). This is not flexible enough for most
schools.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

We are replacing the fixed fees with a fully dynamic fee manager. The school
admin will be able to:

  - Create any number of fee items with custom names (e.g., "Tuition Fee",
    "Computer Lab Fee", "Hostel Fee", "Uniform Levy", "PTA Levy", etc.)
  - Set the amount for each fee item
  - Assign fee items to specific classes (for example, "Laboratory Fee"
    might only apply to SS1, SS2, and SS3, not junior classes)
  - Toggle whether each fee item should appear on the report card or not

This means every school can define their own fee structure, and only the
fees relevant to a particular student's class will appear on that student's
report card. If a school decides they do not want fees on the report card at
all, they can simply turn the entire section off.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. STUDENT POSITIONS — ARM AND OVERALL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

Positions tell parents where their child ranks academically compared to
other students. Currently, the system only calculates a student's position
within their specific section (arm). We are expanding this.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

There will now be two types of positions calculated for every student:

  A) Position in Arm:
     This is the student's rank within their specific class section. For
     example, if a student is in JSS1-A, their arm position tells them
     where they rank among all the students in JSS1-A specifically.

  B) Position in Class (Overall):
     This is the student's rank across the entire class level. For example,
     if JSS1 has three arms — JSS1-A, JSS1-B, and JSS1-C — the overall
     position tells the student where they rank among ALL JSS1 students
     combined.

Both positions will appear on the report card. The school admin can choose
to show one, both, or neither through the settings.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SUBJECT POSITIONS, HIGHEST AND LOWEST SCORES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

For each subject on the report card, the system will show additional
context so parents can understand how their child performed relative to
others.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

Beside each subject's score on the report card, the following will appear:

  - Subject Position: Where the student ranks in that specific subject
    (e.g., "5th out of 40")
  - Highest Score: The best score achieved by any student in that subject
  - Lowest Score: The weakest score in that subject

This gives parents a clear picture. If their child scored 72 in Mathematics
and the highest in the class was 75, the parent can see their child is very
close to the top. If the lowest was 25, it provides further context.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GRADE TALLY (NUMBER OF As, Bs, Cs, etc.)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

A summary count of how many A grades, B grades, C grades, and so on a
student achieved across all their subjects. This is a quick way for parents
and administrators to see the overall quality of a student's performance at
a glance.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

After all subject scores are graded, the system will automatically count
and display a summary like this:

    "6As, 4Bs, 2Cs, 1F"

This means the student got 6 subjects at grade A, 4 at grade B, 2 at grade
C, and 1 failure. This summary will appear on both the individual student's
report card and on the master sheet (the class-wide results overview used by
administrators).


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SUBJECTS OFFERED
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

A simple count showing how many subjects a student took in that term. Not
all students in the same class necessarily offer the same number of
subjects, especially in senior secondary where students choose between
Science, Arts, and Commercial tracks.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

The number of subjects offered will be automatically counted and displayed
on the report card. For example: "Subjects Offered: 12". This helps
parents understand the workload and context of the total and average scores.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PASS / FAIL STATUS (TERMLY)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

At the end of each term (First Term and Second Term), the system will
clearly indicate whether a student has passed or failed that term based on
rules defined by the school.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

The school admin will set up pass/fail rules through a simple settings
page. The rules include:

  - A minimum average percentage to pass (e.g., "Students must average at
    least 40% across all subjects")
  - Mandatory subjects that must be individually passed (e.g., "A student
    must pass both Mathematics and English regardless of their overall
    average")
  - Different rules for different class levels if needed (e.g., SS3 might
    require a 45% minimum while JSS1 requires 40%)

Based on these rules, each student's report card will display either "PASS"
or "FAIL" for that term. This is fully automatic — once the rules are set,
the system handles everything.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. PROMOTION STATUS (THIRD TERM / END OF SESSION)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

At the end of the academic session (Third Term), the system will determine
whether each student is promoted to the next class or not. This is
different from the termly pass/fail because it considers the student's
performance across all three terms combined.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

The promotion decision will be based on the cumulative average of all three
terms. The system will take the student's First Term average, Second Term
average, and Third Term average, combine them, and calculate an overall
session average.

If this session average meets the minimum threshold set by the school
(e.g., 40%), the student is marked as "PROMOTED". If not, they are marked
as "NOT PROMOTED".

Just like the termly pass/fail, the school can also require that certain
mandatory subjects (like Mathematics and English) must be passed across the
session for promotion to apply.

The promotion status will only appear on the Third Term report card, since
promotion decisions only happen at the end of the session.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. ATTENDANCE ON THE REPORT CARD
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

The report card will display how many times the school was open during the
term and how many of those days the student was present, absent, late, or
arrived early. Currently, this information has to be entered manually. We
are automating it.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

The system will calculate attendance automatically using two sources:

  A) Term Calendar (School Days):
     The school admin will set the term start date and end date. They will
     also mark any holidays or special days when the school was closed. The
     system will then automatically calculate how many actual school days
     existed in that term (excluding weekends and holidays). This becomes
     the "Number of Times School Opened" figure.

  B) Student Attendance Records (NFC / Biometric):
     The system already tracks daily student attendance through the NFC card
     scanning and fingerprint features. When a student taps their card or
     scans their fingerprint each morning, the system records their
     presence. At result generation time, the system will count how many
     days each individual student clocked in during the term.

The report card will then show:
  - Times School Opened: e.g., 58 days
  - Times Present: e.g., 55 days
  - Times Absent: e.g., 3 days
  - Times Late: e.g., 2 days
  - Times Early: e.g., 53 days

All of this is fully automatic. No manual counting needed.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. QR CODE VERIFICATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

Each student's report card will contain a small QR code (a square barcode
that can be scanned with any phone camera). This QR code serves as a
security and verification feature.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

When a result is generated, a unique QR code will be placed on the report
card. When scanned, the QR code will display the student's basic result
summary — their name, admission number, class, average score, and position.

This serves two important purposes:

  1. Anti-Forgery: Parents, other schools, or employers can scan the QR
     code to verify that the result is authentic and was genuinely issued by
     the school. It prevents students from creating fake report cards.

  2. Professionalism: QR codes on result sheets are an industry standard
     for modern schools. They make the report card look more official and
     trustworthy.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. KEY TO GRADES (GRADING SCALE)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

A reference table printed on the report card that shows what each grade
means. For example:

    A1 (75-100)  — Excellent
    B2 (70-74)   — Very Good
    B3 (65-69)   — Good
    C4 (60-64)   — Credit
    ...
    F9 (0-39)    — Fail

HOW IT WILL WORK:

The grading scale is already defined in the system's settings (Grade
Boundaries). We will ensure that this scale is always printed on every
report card so that parents can understand what each letter grade means.
The school admin can customise the grade boundaries and labels, and whatever
they set will automatically reflect on all report cards.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. RESULT PUBLISHING CONTROL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

A feature that allows the school admin to control when students and parents
can see their results. Just because results have been entered and generated
does not mean they should be immediately visible to everyone.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

After results are entered and the report cards are generated, they remain
in "unpublished" state by default. Students and parents logging into the
portal will see a message saying "Results are not yet available."

When the school is ready — after all verifications, signatures, and
approvals — the admin clicks a "Publish Results" button for a specific
class and term. Only then will students and parents be able to view and
download their report cards.

If the admin discovers an error after publishing, they can "Unpublish" the
results, make corrections, and publish again. This gives the school full
control over the release of results.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. NOTICE ON THE REPORT CARDAffective & Psychomotor Domains Setup
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

A free-text area where the school admin can type a custom message or notice
that will appear on every student's report card for that term. This is not
the same as the school's general notice board. This is specifically for
important information that the school wants every parent to see when they
receive their child's result.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

In the result settings page, there will be a text box labeled "Report Card
Notice". The admin simply types whatever message they want to communicate.
For example:

  - "Resumption date for Second Term is 13th January, 2025. All students
    must report with their school fees receipt."

  - "Inter-house sports competition will hold on 5th March, 2025. Parents
    are cordially invited."

  - "Outstanding fees must be cleared before the start of next term.
    Defaulters will not be allowed to sit for examinations."

  - "The school management congratulates all students who performed
    excellently this term. Keep up the good work!"

The notice will print in a clearly visible section on the report card,
typically near the bottom, before the signatures. The admin can change the
notice every term, and it will only appear on the report cards generated
for that specific term.

If the admin leaves the notice field empty, no notice section will appear
on the report card. The toggle in the settings panel also allows the admin
to turn the entire notice section on or off.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. CUSTOMIZABLE RESULT SECTIONS (TOGGLE ON/OFF)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

Not every school wants every possible section on their report card. Some
schools do not need attendance displayed. Others do not want fees printed.
Some prefer not to show positions. The system will accommodate all of these
preferences.

HOW IT WILL WORK:

In the result settings page, the admin will see a panel of toggle switches.
Each switch controls whether a specific section appears on the report card
or not. The available toggles include:

  - Show/Hide Admission Number
  - Show/Hide Position in Arm
  - Show/Hide Position in Class (Overall)
  - Show/Hide Subjects Offered
  - Show/Hide Grade Tally
  - Show/Hide Term Status (Pass/Fail)
  - Show/Hide Promotion Status
  - Show/Hide Attendance Section
  - Show/Hide School Fees
  - Show/Hide QR Code
  - Show/Hide Grading Key
  - Show/Hide Affective Domains (Behaviour Assessment)
  - Show/Hide Psychomotor Domains (Physical Skills Assessment)
  - Show/Hide Teacher's Comment
  - Show/Hide Head Teacher's Comment
  - Show/Hide Principal's Comment
  - Show/Hide Report Card Notice
  - Show/Hide School Stamp Area
  - Show/Hide Closing Date
  - Show/Hide Next Term Begins Date

This makes the system extremely flexible. Every school can build their
ideal report card by simply toggling the sections they want.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. TEMPLATE SELECTION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

The visual design and layout of the report card. Different schools prefer
different looks. The system already offers multiple report card designs, and
all of the features described above will work with every design.

AVAILABLE DESIGNS:

  1. Classic Style — A traditional, clean report card layout
  2. Modern Aesthetic — A contemporary design with modern fonts and spacing
  3. Corporate Form — A formal, institution-style layout
  4. Elegant Series — A premium, decorative design
  5. Visual Chart — Includes performance graphs and visual indicators

The school admin selects their preferred template from the settings, and all
generated report cards will use that design. They can switch templates at
any time, and all the data (scores, positions, fees, attendance, etc.) will
automatically adapt to the new layout.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. MASTER SHEET UPGRADES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS:

The master sheet is the administrative view that shows all students' results
in one large table. It is used by teachers and administrators for
record-keeping and analysis, not given to parents. We are upgrading it to
include all the new data points.

WHAT WILL CHANGE:

The master sheet will now include:
  - Admission Number column
  - Grade Tally column (e.g., "6A, 3B, 2C, 1F")
  - Subjects Offered count
  - Promotion Status (on the Third Term / Cumulative master sheet)
  - All existing columns (Position, Total, Average, Grade, Remark) remain

The master sheet can be exported to Excel/CSV and printed in landscape
format for physical filing.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. WHAT STAYS THE SAME
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following features already exist and work well. They will not be
changed, only improved to work seamlessly with the new additions:

  - School Logo, Name, Address, and Motto on the report card header
  - Student Photo on the report card
  - Teacher's Comment, Head Teacher's Comment, and Principal's Comment
  - Signature uploads for signatories
  - Affective Domains (Behaviour Assessment — Punctuality, Neatness, etc.)
  - Psychomotor Domains (Physical Skills — Handwriting, Sports, etc.)
  - Grade Boundaries configuration
  - Grading Structure and Component Weights (CA, Exam breakdown)
  - Print-to-PDF functionality
  - Notices module (the general school noticeboard remains separate)


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

In summary, after this upgrade, every student's report card will be able to
display the following (all optional and toggleable by the school):

  1.  School branding (logo, name, address, motto)
  2.  Student photo and personal details
  3.  Admission / Registration number
  4.  Class and section
  5.  Term and academic session
  6.  Subject scores with CA breakdown
  7.  Subject grade and remark per subject
  8.  Subject position (rank in each subject)
  9.  Highest and lowest score per subject
  10. Total score and average percentage
  11. Position in Arm (section rank)
  12. Position in Class (overall rank across all arms)
  13. Number of subjects offered
  14. Grade tally (count of As, Bs, Cs, etc.)
  15. Term status (Pass or Fail)
  16. Promotion status (Promoted or Not Promoted — Third Term only)
  17. Attendance summary (days opened, present, absent, late, early)
  18. School fees breakdown for next term
  19. QR code for result verification
  20. Key to grades (grading scale reference)
  21. Affective domain assessment
  22. Psychomotor domain assessment
  23. Teacher's comment
  24. Head Teacher's comment
  25. Principal's comment
  26. Report card notice (admin-typed message for parents)
  27. Signatory names and signatures
  28. School stamp area
  29. Closing date and next term resumption date

All of this will be built in phases, tested thoroughly, and rolled out
without disrupting any existing functionality.
